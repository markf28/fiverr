Our Story

A Family Tradition
Across Generations

La Stella was born from a single belief: that the finest Italian cooking is an act of love. Founded by Chef Marco Ferretti in 1987 after a decade in the kitchens of Milan and Florence, our restaurant has remained a family affair — his daughter Sofia now leads the kitchen, weaving her own creative voice into dishes you'll recognise from the first bite.

Every plate tells a story. Our pasta is rolled each morning. Our sauces simmer for hours. Our produce arrives from the same family farms it always has. This is not convenience cooking — it is a devotion to craft, passed from hand to hand.

✦
Sofia Ferretti Executive Chef & Co-Owner
